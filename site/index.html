How these numbers are made

Fit = GGUF file size (read from Hugging Face) + KV cache (computed from each model's config; sliding-window and linear-attention layers handled) + ~1 GB overhead per GPU. Speed is an estimated upper-bound-style figure: memory bandwidth × efficiency ÷ bytes read per token. Where we have benchmark data for your hardware, the badge says measured (median of real runs) or scaled from measured (a measurement of a same-size or same-model-different-quant run, scaled by bytes read); otherwise it is estimated and not a benchmark. Estimated speeds use an efficiency factor calibrated to measured runs. Real results vary with backend, drivers and context. Ranking is "fits and is fast enough first, then bigger", a heuristic, not a quality benchmark. Assumptions are listed in hardware.json.
