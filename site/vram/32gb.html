FAQ

What is the best local LLM for 32GB VRAM?

By size, Flint 40B-A2.5B UD-Q5_K_XL is the largest model in our dataset that fits fully at 16k context (30.4 GB, ~391 tok/s (scaled from measured)).

How many models fit in 32GB VRAM?

25 of 30 models fit fully at 16k context with an f16 KV cache; 1 more run only with CPU offload.

How much context can I use with 32GB?

It depends on the model: for Flint 40B-A2.5B the KV cache allows up to 32k tokens at f16 and 128k with a q4_0 cache.
