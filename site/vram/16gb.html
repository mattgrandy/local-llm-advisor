FAQ

What is the best local LLM for 16GB VRAM?

By size, gpt-oss 20B Q8_0 is the largest model in our dataset that fits fully at 16k context (12.5 GB, ~189 tok/s (scaled from measured)).

How many models fit in 16GB VRAM?

5 of 13 models fit fully at 16k context with an f16 KV cache; 6 more run only with CPU offload.

How much context can I use with 16GB?

It depends on the model: for gpt-oss 20B the KV cache allows up to 64k tokens at f16 and 128k with a q4_0 cache.
