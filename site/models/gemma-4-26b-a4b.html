FAQ

How much VRAM does Gemma 4 26B-A4B need?

The smallest quant (Q4_K_M) is 16.95 GB, so you need roughly 18 GB for an 8k context including KV cache and overhead. At 256k context the f16 KV cache alone is 5.6 GB.

Can I run Gemma 4 26B-A4B on a RTX 5090 32GB?

Yes: on RTX 5090 32GB it fits fully as Q8_0 at about 217 tok/s (measured). 20 of 29 hardware configurations fit it fully at 16k context.

What is the maximum context for Gemma 4 26B-A4B?

256k tokens natively. Memory, not the model, is usually the limit: see the KV cache table above.
