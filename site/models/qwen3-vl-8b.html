FAQ

How much VRAM does Qwen3-VL 8B need?

The smallest quant (Q4_K_M) is 5.03 GB, so you need roughly 7 GB for an 8k context including KV cache and overhead. At 1M context the f16 KV cache alone is 147.5 GB.

Can I run Qwen3-VL 8B on a RTX 5090 32GB?

Yes: on RTX 5090 32GB it fits fully as Q8_0 at about 167 tok/s (measured). 27 of 28 hardware configurations fit it fully at 16k context.

What is the maximum context for Qwen3-VL 8B?

256k tokens natively, up to 1M with YaRN as documented on the model card. Memory, not the model, is usually the limit: see the KV cache table above.
