Short answer

Yes. Gemma 4 31B Q4_K_M fits fully on RTX 4090 24GB at 16k context, using 20.5 of 23.0 GB usable, and generates about 44.4 tokens/s in our own run. As the context fills it slows down: 40.1 tok/s with 16k tokens already in context (90% of the empty-context speed), measured. For Q4_K_M, our bandwidth-only estimate was ~30 tok/s against a measured median of ~44 (32% too pessimistic).

FAQ

How fast is Gemma 4 31B on RTX 4090 24GB?

About 44.4 tokens/s for generation (Q4_K_M on llama.cpp CUDA, 128-token generation), 2403 tokens/s for prompt processing. Speed drops as the context fills; see the depth table.

How much memory does Gemma 4 31B need on RTX 4090 24GB?

The advisor's pick, Q4_K_M, is 18.3 GB of weights plus 2.18 GB of KV cache at 16k context (20.5 GB), against 23.0 GB usable.

Is the Gemma 4 31B speed on RTX 4090 24GB measured or estimated?

Measured: our own llama-bench run on a rented GeForce RTX 4090, including runs with 4k to 16k tokens already in context.

What can I use instead of RTX 4090 24GB for Gemma 4 31B?

With less memory, Mac mini M6 32GB (~6 tok/s), MacBook Air M5 32GB (~5 tok/s) also fit it fully. Faster: RTX 5090 32GB (~55 tok/s).

What is the maximum context for Gemma 4 31B on RTX 4090 24GB?

With an f16 KV cache about 32k tokens, 64k with q8_0 and 128k with q4_0, bounded by the model's own limit.
