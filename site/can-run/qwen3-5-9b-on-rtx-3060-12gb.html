Short answer

Yes. Qwen3.5 9B Q8_0 fits fully on RTX 3060 12GB (32GB RAM) at 16k context, using 10.1 of 11.0 GB usable, and generates about 36.6 tokens/s in our own run. For Q8_0, our bandwidth-only estimate was ~21 tok/s against a measured median of ~37 (43% too pessimistic).

FAQ

How fast is Qwen3.5 9B on RTX 3060 12GB (32GB RAM)?

About 36.6 tokens/s for generation (Q8_0 on llama.cpp CUDA, 128-token generation), 1945 tokens/s for prompt processing. Speed drops as the context fills; see the depth table.

How much memory does Qwen3.5 9B need on RTX 3060 12GB (32GB RAM)?

The advisor's pick, Q8_0, is 9.5 GB of weights plus 0.59 GB of KV cache at 16k context (10.1 GB), against 11.0 GB usable.

What is the maximum context for Qwen3.5 9B on RTX 3060 12GB (32GB RAM)?

With an f16 KV cache about 32k tokens, 64k with q8_0 and 128k with q4_0, bounded by the model's own limit.
