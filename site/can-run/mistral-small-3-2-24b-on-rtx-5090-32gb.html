Short answer

Yes. Mistral Small 3.2 24B Q8_0 fits fully on RTX 5090 32GB at 16k context, using 27.7 of 31.0 GB usable, and generates about 63.9 tokens/s in our own run. As the context fills it slows down: 57.4 tok/s with 16k tokens already in context (90% of the empty-context speed), measured. For Q8_0, our bandwidth-only estimate was ~39 tok/s against a measured median of ~64 (38% too pessimistic).

FAQ

How fast is Mistral Small 3.2 24B on RTX 5090 32GB?

About 63.9 tokens/s for generation (Q8_0 on llama.cpp CUDA, 128-token generation), 5246 tokens/s for prompt processing. Speed drops as the context fills; see the depth table.

How much memory does Mistral Small 3.2 24B need on RTX 5090 32GB?

The advisor's pick, Q8_0, is 25.1 GB of weights plus 2.68 GB of KV cache at 16k context (27.7 GB), against 31.0 GB usable.

Is the Mistral Small 3.2 24B speed on RTX 5090 32GB measured or estimated?

Measured: our own llama-bench run on a rented GeForce RTX 5090, including runs with 4k to 16k tokens already in context.

What can I use instead of RTX 5090 32GB for Mistral Small 3.2 24B?

With less memory, Mac mini M6 32GB (~7 tok/s), MacBook Air M5 32GB (~6 tok/s), RTX 4090 24GB (~46 tok/s) also fit it fully.

What is the maximum context for Mistral Small 3.2 24B on RTX 5090 32GB?

With an f16 KV cache about 32k tokens, 64k with q8_0 and 64k with q4_0, bounded by the model's own limit.
