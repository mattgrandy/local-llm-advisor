Verdict from the numbers

  • Fit. RTX 3090 24GB fits 20 of 30 models fully at 16k context (largest: Qwen3.6 35B-A3B UD-Q4_K_M); RTX 5090 32GB fits 25 (largest: Flint 40B-A2.5B UD-Q5_K_XL). Only RTX 5090 32GB fits fully: Nemotron 3 Nano 30B-A3B, Nemotron 3.5 Lightning 30B-A3B, DeepSeek R1 Distill Qwen 32B, Flint 40B-A2.5B, Nanbeige4.3 33B.
  • Speed. On the 20 models that fit fully on both, RTX 5090 32GB is faster by a typical 1.7x. RTX 3090 24GB is ahead on 0, RTX 5090 32GB on 20. 7 of those comparisons use measured speeds on both sides; the rest involve at least one estimate (see the badges).
  • Trade-off. RTX 5090 32GB has 8.0 GB more usable memory (23.0 vs 31.0 GB) and higher bandwidth than the other (1792 vs 936 GB/s).
