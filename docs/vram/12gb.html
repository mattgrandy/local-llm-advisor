FAQ

What is the best local LLM for 12GB VRAM?

By size, Gemma 4 12B Q6_K is the largest model in our dataset that fits fully at 16k context (10.4 GB, ~43 tok/s (scaled from measured)).

How many models fit in 12GB VRAM?

8 of 28 models fit fully at 16k context with an f16 KV cache; 15 more run only with CPU offload.

How much context can I use with 12GB?

It depends on the model: for Gemma 4 12B the KV cache allows up to 32k tokens at f16 and 128k with a q4_0 cache.
