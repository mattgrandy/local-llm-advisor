Short answer

Yes. gpt-oss 20B Q8_0 fits fully on RTX 5070 Ti 16GB at 16k context, using 12.5 of 15.0 GB usable, and generates about 223.8 tokens/s in our own run. As the context fills it slows down: 198.9 tok/s with 16k tokens already in context (89% of the empty-context speed), measured. For Q8_0, our bandwidth-only estimate was ~236 tok/s against a measured median of ~224 (6% too optimistic).

FAQ

How fast is gpt-oss 20B on RTX 5070 Ti 16GB?

About 223.8 tokens/s for generation (Q8_0 on llama.cpp CUDA, 128-token generation), 9839 tokens/s for prompt processing. Speed drops as the context fills; see the depth table.

How much memory does gpt-oss 20B need on RTX 5070 Ti 16GB?

The advisor's pick, Q8_0, is 12.1 GB of weights plus 0.41 GB of KV cache at 16k context (12.5 GB), against 15.0 GB usable.

Is the gpt-oss 20B speed on RTX 5070 Ti 16GB measured or estimated?

Measured: our own llama-bench run on a rented GeForce RTX 5070 Ti, including runs with 4k to 16k tokens already in context.

What can I use instead of RTX 5070 Ti 16GB for gpt-oss 20B?

Nothing with less memory in our list fits it fully. Faster: RTX 4090 24GB (~258 tok/s), RTX 5090 32GB (~375 tok/s), RTX PRO 6000 96GB (~376 tok/s).

What is the maximum context for gpt-oss 20B on RTX 5070 Ti 16GB?

With an f16 KV cache about 64k tokens, 128k with q8_0 and 128k with q4_0, bounded by the model's own limit.
