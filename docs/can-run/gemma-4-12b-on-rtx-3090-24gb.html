Short answer

Yes. Gemma 4 12B Q8_0 fits fully on RTX 3090 24GB at 16k context, using 13.7 of 23.0 GB usable, and generates about 57.2 tokens/s in our own run. As the context fills it slows down: 54.3 tok/s with 16k tokens already in context (95% of the empty-context speed), measured. For Q8_0, our bandwidth-only estimate was ~39 tok/s against a measured median of ~57 (32% too pessimistic).

FAQ

How fast is Gemma 4 12B on RTX 3090 24GB?

About 57.2 tokens/s for generation (Q8_0 on llama.cpp CUDA, 128-token generation), 3408 tokens/s for prompt processing. Speed drops as the context fills; see the depth table.

How much memory does Gemma 4 12B need on RTX 3090 24GB?

The advisor's pick, Q8_0, is 13.1 GB of weights plus 0.60 GB of KV cache at 16k context (13.7 GB), against 23.0 GB usable.

Is the Gemma 4 12B speed on RTX 3090 24GB measured or estimated?

Measured: our own llama-bench run on a rented GeForce RTX 3090, including runs with 4k to 128k tokens already in context.

What can I use instead of RTX 3090 24GB for Gemma 4 12B?

With less memory, Mac mini / iMac M4 16GB (~11 tok/s), RTX 5070 12GB (~51 tok/s), RTX 4070 12GB (~43 tok/s) also fit it fully. Faster: RTX 5090 32GB (~104 tok/s), RTX PRO 6000 96GB (~101 tok/s).

What is the maximum context for Gemma 4 12B on RTX 3090 24GB?

With an f16 KV cache about 256k tokens, 256k with q8_0 and 256k with q4_0, bounded by the model's own limit.
